# ADR-005: Human-reviewed drafts only

## Status

Accepted

## Date

2026-07-17

## Context

Outreach and proposal narratives are useful, but the concept has no approval to connect to company communication accounts or represent external commitments.

## Decision

The app generates text drafts and downloadable Markdown reports. It has no email connector, service account, Send button, auto-submit action, or automatic bid/no-bid workflow.

## Alternatives considered

- **Power Automate/email delivery:** rejected until leadership, IT, security, legal/privacy, and communications approvals exist.
- **No drafting:** rejected because “what should we say?” is part of the intended manager workflow.

## Consequences

Users review and move content through approved channels. The demo proves decision and content assistance, not external automation.
