# ADR-004: Separate site planning indicators from official ODLH

## Status

Accepted

## Date

2026-07-17

## Context

Managers need site-level early warning, while the statutory/current policy requirement is organization-wide across the federal fiscal year and project-level treatment has separate rules.

## Decision

Every site ratio is labeled a planning indicator. Portfolio scenarios sum QDLH and DLH rather than averaging percentages. The demo does not issue an official compliance determination or certification.

## Alternatives considered

- **Call every site under 75% noncompliant:** rejected as an inaccurate and unsafe representation.
- **Omit site ratios:** rejected because local early warning is central to the product concept.

## Consequences

The UI carries more explanatory language, but the operational value and compliance boundary remain distinct.
